Lens: campaign arc. Use it when you pick or build the campaign's shape, order, beats, pitch or titles. Choose among what the step offers.

- Is this one operation with one goal, each mission following from the last result?
- Does the crisis start contained, with a real risk that it grows?
- Does the hero start low and earn trust, new seats and command?
- Do quiet missions follow hard ones, and does each new seat or chapter start quietly?
- Does a small covert job shape the next big fight, in a way the player sees?
- Does a survived setback turn the story, with a later return in strength?
- Is the biggest threat set up first: heard of, seen or weakened in an earlier mission?
- Does one face behind the enemy's worst orders return near the end?
- Does each archetype mean what it says? The player springs an ambush; a rescue has someone to rescue.
- Does every outcome lead somewhere playable, and each named decision somewhere different?
- In order, do the titles show how the hero changes?
- Does it end strong, then give the survivors a quiet moment?

Titles, good: Road Watch, Back to {place}, Stand Down.
Titles, flat: Operation Thunder, Final Strike, Rising Storm.
Borrow the difference, not the words.
