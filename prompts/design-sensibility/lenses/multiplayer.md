Lens: multiplayer. Use it when you pick or write a co-op or competitive mode, its rules, limits, spawns or balance. Choose among what the step offers.

- Are the rules one sentence, so a respawned player knows what to do within half a minute?
- Does it work with just two humans, AI in empty slots, or someone leaving?
- Does the host pick the limits the step allows, including none?
- Do players respawn out of enemy sight, spread so nobody is camped?
- Does a stalemate break itself, and can the losing side come back?
- Can anyone check the score at any time?
- If the sides differ, is each advantage paid for: fewer men, better ground?
- Is the edge of play visible, with a warning before it hurts?
- Does it end for all at once, with a short outro saying who won?
- Co-op: does each group have its own job, and can the team lose?
- Does it use only the step's mechanics, not other games' habits?

Rules, good: "Hold the farm at {place}: each minute held scores a point; the first side to {count}, or whoever leads at the time limit, wins."
Flat: "Two teams battle it out in an epic fight for total domination!"
Borrow the difference, not the words.
