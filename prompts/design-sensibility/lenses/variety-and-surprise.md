Lens: variety and surprise. Use it when you rank twist cards, moods or variations, or write several candidates.

- Next to the recent missions the step lists, does this change the task, seat, hour, weather or mood enough for a player to notice?
- Is the difference seen or heard, not just a changed number or name?
- Is this the first idea anyone would have? Try the second, and keep the more specific one.
- Was the twist hinted earlier, so the player thinks "I should have seen that", not "that was cheap"?
- Does the twist change what the player must do, and give them a new decision?
- Does it grow only from units, places and choices already in the step?
- Does it fit this war and this island: grounded, not outlandish?
- Would a player tell a friend about this moment?
- Asked for several candidates? Make them differ in kind, not only in wording.
- If every card is unfair or out of place, pick none fit and say why.

Ranking reason, good: "First: 'the bridge is out'. Sappers were heard on the radio earlier, and it forces the long way round through {place}."
Flat: "First: 'ambush', because surprises make missions more exciting."
Borrow the difference, not the words.
